# ultimatum-game

Ultimatum Game: social economic decision-making task involving fairness, reward, and social evaluation

In this game, participants act as responders and decide whether to accept or reject a monetary offer proposed by another player. On each trial, participants first view a fixation cross, followed by a short video of the proposer and information about the total amount offered. The proposed division is then presented, and participants indicate whether they accept or reject the offer. If accepted, both players receive the proposed shares; if rejected, neither player receives any money. In this adapted version of the UG, two male proposers were used. Their facial cues consisted of directed/long gaze and averted gaze, which were associated with different offer proportions. Four total amounts (€10, €7.50, €2.50, and €0.20) and five offer proportions were presented. Feedback was provided after each decision. 
Task created for an fMRI experiment.
